## The Recipe

- main tools [Krita](https://krita.org) + [Inkscape](https://inkscape.org), both free.
- font i use [Mochiy Pop One (Regular)](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Mochiy+Pop+One), OFL.
- color palletes taken from [Rosé Pine](https://rosepinetheme.com/).
